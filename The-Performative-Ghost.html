The Performative Ghost

The death of the authentic self in the age of broadcasting.

You are no longer a person; you are a brand managing a lifelong public relations crisis. You curate your aesthetic, you police your tone, you broadcast your virtues to an audience of strangers.

The individual has died, replaced by a digital avatar that demands constant feeding. You are a ghost haunting your own algorithm, performing authenticity for engagement metrics. You have internalized the panopticon so deeply that you do not believe you exist unless you are being perceived. To be unseen is to cease to be.
