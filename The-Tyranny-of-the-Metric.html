The Tyranny of the Metric

The reduction of human experience to measurable data.

Once you measure a behavior, you destroy its soul. A metric is an algorithm that incentivizes optimization over truth. We no longer act to be good; we act to hit the KPIs of our existence.

Friendship is measured in likes, success in followers, intelligence in standardized scores. When a metric becomes the target, it ceases to be a good measure. We are optimizing our lives for algorithms that care nothing for our humanity, trading the unquantifiable depth of living for the shallow certainty of a dashboard.
