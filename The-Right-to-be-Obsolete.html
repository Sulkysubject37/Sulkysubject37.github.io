The Right to be Obsolete

The liberation of irrelevance.

We are terrified of being left behind, of becoming irrelevant in the relentless churn of updates. But obsolescence is not a failure; it is a profound liberation. To be obsolete is to step off the treadmill of forced, continuous relevance.

It is to accept that you do not need to update your operating system, that you do not need to adapt to every new metric of success. The right to be obsolete is the right to remain human in a system that demands you become a machine. It is the ultimate act of defiance: to simply stop participating, and let the future happen without you.
